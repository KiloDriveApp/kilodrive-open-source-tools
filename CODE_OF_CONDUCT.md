# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be made privately through the repository's maintainers or GitHub reporting mechanisms. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
